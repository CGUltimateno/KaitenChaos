# esp-26-kaiten-chaos

## Guide

In this game, you play as a sushi chef. Each day, customers come in and place "vibe-based" orders. Instead of directly ordering a sushi roll with specific ingredients, a customer might ask you to "keep it classic", for example.

### 1. Restaurant View

After pressing Play in the main menu, you will be directed to the restaurant view.

Here, you can:

* See the customers.
* Take orders by clicking on the speech bubbles above the customers.
* View your collected orders by clicking the ORDERS button in the top-left corner.

The Orders View also tells you how many ingredients you should use for each roll.

For example:

- 2× Classic Phrase → Use 2 ingredients with a good Classic trait score.

### 2. Ingredients

Click the Ingredients button at the bottom of the screen to enter the fridge view.

Here, you can select the ingredients for your sushi roll:

* Click an ingredient to select it.
* You can choose a maximum of 6 ingredients.
* Once you are satisfied with your selection, click Confirm to proceed to the sushi assembly.

### 3. Assembly Station

To prepare the sushi:

1. Drag and drop the rice bowl onto the sushi mat, where the nori is already placed.
2. Drag and drop your selected ingredients onto the rice.
3. To move on to the cutting station, drag the unrolled sushi on the mat to the right edge of the screen.

You will then be taken to the cutting station.

### 4. Cutting Station

At the cutting station:

1. Click and drag the sushi to roll it.
2. Once the sushi is rolled, click on it to place your cuts.
3. Ideally, make 5 cuts, resulting in 6 sushi pieces.
4. Try to make each cut as evenly spaced as possible.
5. Click Confirm to proceed to plating.

### 5. Serving Station

To serve your sushi:

1. Open the Orders View.
2. Find the ticket corresponding to the customer you want to serve.
3. Drag and drop the ticket onto the box on the right side of the plate.
4. Drag and drop the plate onto the conveyor belt.

If you are unsatisfied with your roll, you can also drag and drop it into the trash can.

---

## Other Features

### Guidebook

The guidebook can be accessed by clicking the book icon in the top-right corner of the screen.

* The first page shows ingredient icons and their names.
* The following pages provide information about the traits of ingredients you have discovered.
* At the beginning, every trait for an ingredient is shown as locked.
* After you use an ingredient for a relevant order, its trait information becomes available.

For example, after trying mango for a Sweet order, you can check the guidebook to see how well mango fits the Sweet trait.

### Day Timer

The clock at the top-center of the screen shows how much time is left in the current day.
