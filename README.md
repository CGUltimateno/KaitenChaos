# esp-26-kaiten-chaos

## Guide:

- In this game you play as a sushi chef. Each day customers come in and place "vibe-based" orders. For example instead of a direct order of a sushi roll with cucumber and salmon, the customer may ask for you to "keep it classic".

1. Restaurant View: After pressing play in the main menu you will be directed to the restaurant view. Here you can see customers and take orders by clicking on the speech bubbles on customers. After a bubble has been clicked you can see it in the orders tab, which is reachable by clicking the 'ORDERS' button on the top left. This is the Orders View:
    - Orders View also tells you the amount of ingredients you should use for the roll. For example: 2x (Classic Phrase) --> 2 ingredients with a good 'classic' trait score
2. Ingredients: By clicking on the 'Ingredients' button on the bottom you will be directed to the fridge view where you can select the ingredients for your roll by clicking on the ingredient you want. You can choose a max. of 6 ingredients. Click the confirm button to move on to the sushi assembly, once you're satisfied with your selection of ingredients.
3. Assembly Station: To prepare the sushi:
    1. Drag and drop rice bowl on to the sushi mat (where the nori is already placed).
    2. Drag and drop your ingredients on the rice.
To move on to the cutting station drag the unrolled sushi on the mat to the right edge of the screen. You will then be directed to the cutting station.
4. Cutting station: click and drag the sushi to roll it. Then on the rolled sushi click on where you want to make a cut. Ideally, there should be 5 cuts (so resulting in 6 rolls) with each cut being as even as possible. Click confirm to move on to plating.
5. Serving station: Open the Orders View and click and drag the ticket you wish to serve your sushi to to the box on the right side of the plate. Then drag and drop the plate on the belt.
If you're unsatisfied with your roll, you can also drag and drop it to the trash can.

Other:
- The guidebook is accessible by clicking the book icon to the top right of the screen. The first page shows ingredient icons with ingredient names. The other pages provide insight to the traits of ingredients you've discovered so far. In the beginning every trait for an ingredient appears locked. After you try mango for a sweet order, for example, you can check the guidebook again and there you will see how fitting the ingredient is to the trait.
- The clock in the middle at the top shows how much time is left in the day.
